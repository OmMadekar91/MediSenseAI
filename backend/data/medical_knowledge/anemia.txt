Title: Anemia - Diagnosis
AccessDate: 2026-10-10
---

Anemia occurs when the blood lacks enough healthy red blood cells. Hemoglobin is the protein in red blood cells that carries oxygen. A primary marker for anemia is low hemoglobin levels. Typical adult reference ranges are 14-17 gm/dL for men and 12-15 gm/dL for women.
